
MARS MISSION KNOWLEDGE BASE
Location: Mount Sharp, Gale Crater, Mars

WHAT IS BOXWORK?
Boxwork is a network of intersecting, erosion-resistant
mineral ridges within rock.

WHY IS BOXWORK IMPORTANT?
These structures can form when mineral-rich water moves
through fractures in rock and deposits minerals.
Later erosion can expose the harder mineral-filled
fractures as raised ridges.

WHY STUDY BOXWORK ON MARS?
Boxwork-like structures may preserve evidence of past
groundwater activity on Mars.

Studying their geometry, mineral composition, and
surrounding rocks can help scientists investigate
the history of water and environmental conditions.
